How it works

A simple parking decision loop.

Parkio is designed to reduce uncertainty before and after arrival while keeping the driver in control.

Discover

Open the map and review nearby community signals and supported parking facilities.

Compare

Use distance, freshness, source, and availability context to decide which option is most useful.

Verify or report

Help keep parking intelligence current by confirming a signal or reporting what changed.

Navigate & return

Continue your trip and use Parkio's return-oriented tools to keep parking context manageable.

Data & trust

Useful signals, clearly sourced.

Parkio separates community observations from municipal facility data and communicates freshness so drivers can understand what they are looking at.

Important: Parking availability can change quickly. Parkio provides decision support, not a guarantee that a space will still be available when you arrive.

Source-aware

Community and municipal signals are treated as distinct data sources rather than blended into an unexplained score.

Freshness matters

Signals can age, be verified, or change status so old information is less likely to be mistaken for current availability.

Municipal data where available

Facility and occupancy information is shown only where a supported public source provides usable data.

No implied partnership

Displaying publicly available municipal data does not by itself imply a partnership, endorsement, or official affiliation.

FAQ

Clear answers about the beta.

What is Parkio?

Parkio is a parking intelligence product that combines community parking signals with available municipal parking data to help drivers make better-informed parking decisions.

Is Parkio live?

Yes. Parkio is currently available as a public web beta at app.parkio.dev. The product is still early-stage, so coverage and features will continue to evolve.

Does Parkio guarantee a parking space?

No. Parking availability changes quickly. Parkio provides signals and context to support a driver's decision, but it cannot guarantee that a space or facility will remain available.

Is Parkio a parking operator or municipality?

No. Parkio is an independent parking technology product. Where municipal data is displayed, it is presented with source context and does not imply an official partnership unless explicitly stated.

Is the beta free to use?

The current public web beta can be accessed without a paid subscription. Future pricing, if any, will be communicated before it applies.

How can I contact Parkio?

Email info@parkio.dev for product, business, beta, privacy, or technical inquiries.
