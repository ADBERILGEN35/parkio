Data & privacy

Useful information, clearly sourced.

Parkio distinguishes municipal facility data from authenticated community workflows and communicates freshness so drivers can understand the limits of the information.

Important: Parking availability can change quickly. Parkio provides decision support, not a guarantee that a space will still be available when you arrive.

Source-aware

Published municipal data includes a readable source label and attribution.

Freshness matters

Availability is withheld when the product's publication policy no longer considers it suitable to show.

Bounded publication

The prepared public experience is read-only, geographically bounded, and intentionally limited.

No implied partnership

Displaying public municipal data does not by itself imply partnership, endorsement, or official affiliation.

FAQ

Clear answers about the current stage.

What is Parkio?

Parkio is a parking discovery and intelligence product that brings supported municipal facility data and authenticated parking workflows into a focused web experience.

What is live today?

Parkio's production infrastructure and web product are live. Registration remains closed and controlled. The dedicated read-only public explore experience is implemented but remains disabled until its final operational and data-rights gates are completed.

Does Parkio guarantee a parking space?

No. Parking availability changes quickly. Parkio provides source and freshness context to support a driver's decision, but it cannot guarantee that a space or facility will remain available.

Is Parkio a parking operator or municipality?

No. Parkio is an independent parking technology product. Displaying public municipal data does not imply an official partnership unless one is explicitly established.

How does Parkio plan to make money?

No paid offer is currently presented. Parkio is evaluating optional premium convenience features and aggregated, privacy-conscious parking-intelligence services after product validation.

How can I contact Parkio?

Email info@parkio.dev for product, business, privacy, data-source, or technical inquiries.
