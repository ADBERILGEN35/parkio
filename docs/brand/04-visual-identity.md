# Visual Identity

## Visual Direction

Parkio should look modern, calm, bright, and practical. The system should balance mobility/product clarity with community warmth and technical credibility.

## Core Visual Signals

- Parking spot geometry.
- Route and arrival cues.
- Location precision without looking like a map clone.
- Community confirmation and trust.
- Clean data and operational reliability.

## Shape Language

- Rounded corners: moderate, not bubbly.
- Cards: light, purposeful, and content-led.
- Lines: clean route/path lines with subtle bends.
- Symbols: simple parking, pin, check, shield, clock, camera, and people motifs.
- Avoid dense map tiles as a dominant brand texture.

## Layout Feel

- Spacious but not empty.
- Information-first.
- Strong hierarchy.
- Calm surfaces.
- Clear calls to action.
- Minimal decoration.

## Visual Motifs

Allowed:

- Subtle street-grid patterns.
- Thin route lines.
- Verified spot markers.
- Photo thumbnail frames.
- Community signal clusters.
- Safety and privacy indicators.

Avoid:

- Neon gradients.
- Dark hacker grids.
- Taxi yellow/black dominance.
- Crypto token shapes.
- Game badges as primary identity.
- Heavy corporate blue dashboards.

## Asset Principles

Every asset should communicate one of these:

- A real parking decision.
- A trusted community signal.
- A privacy-conscious product behavior.
- A technically credible beta.
