# Brand Voice

## Voice Principles

### Clear

Use plain language. Explain what Parkio does in one sentence before adding detail.

### Honest

Name the current stage accurately. Do not soften important limitations with vague optimism.

### Useful

Focus on the driver problem, beta objective, or reviewer question being answered.

### Human

Sound like a responsible product team, not a corporate press release.

## Voice Examples

Use:

- "Find recent, photo-backed parking signals from nearby drivers."
- "Parkio is in hosted-beta preparation."
- "The application layer is certified for hosted-beta use; the live beta is not yet deployed."
- "Market size is TBD."

Avoid:

- "The future of parking is here."
- "Guaranteed parking, anytime."
- "AI-powered citywide parking domination."
- "Trusted by thousands."
- "Production-ready at scale."

## Tone by Context

### Landing Page

Concise, confident, calm, and direct.

### Pitch Deck

Sharper business framing, clear risk, clear next milestone.

### Technical Due Diligence

Evidence-heavy and precise. Link to certification and operations docs.

### Beta Communication

Friendly, transparent, and specific about what testers should try.

### Social Media

Simple product updates, beta progress, and learning notes. Avoid hype.

## Standard Stage Copy

Short:

Parkio is in release-candidate / hosted-beta preparation.

Full:

Parkio is at `v1.0.0-rc1`. The application layer is certified for hosted-beta use. The live hosted beta is not yet deployed, and public production remains not ready.
