# Iconography

## Icon Style

Use simple outline icons with consistent stroke width. Icons should feel useful and functional, not decorative.

Recommended style:

- 24 px base grid.
- 2 px stroke.
- Rounded caps and joins.
- Minimal filled icons only for selected states or status badges.

## Preferred Icon Themes

- Parking.
- Location.
- Search.
- Camera.
- Verification.
- Claim.
- Report.
- Shield/privacy.
- Bell/notifications.
- Clock/Smart Return.
- Community/users.
- Route/path.

## Usage Rules

- Pair unfamiliar icons with labels or tooltips.
- Do not use color alone for status.
- Keep icon metaphors consistent across web, mobile, deck, and landing page.
- Avoid overly detailed map, car, or city icons.
- Avoid trophy/game icons as primary brand symbols; gamification is a support feature, not the brand core.

## Status Icon Recommendations

- Verified: check inside circle or shield.
- Claimed: pin/check or flag.
- Restricted: alert triangle.
- Reported: flag or alert circle.
- Private/secure: shield.
- Smart Return: clock plus route cue.

## Marketing Icons

Marketing section icons should be slightly larger, but still simple:

- 32 px or 40 px.
- Same stroke style.
- Single accent color per icon.
- No complex illustrations inside icons.
