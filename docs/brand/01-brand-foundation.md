# Brand Foundation

## Brand Idea

Parkio makes parking information feel less uncertain by turning fresh driver observations into trusted community intelligence.

## Promise

Parkio helps drivers make better parking decisions with recent, photo-backed, community-verified parking signals.

## Audience

Primary audience:

- Drivers who regularly search for parking in dense, campus, commuter, or visitor-heavy areas.

Secondary audience:

- Beta testers.
- Cloud startup program reviewers.
- Angel investors and early-stage VCs.
- Technical due diligence reviewers.
- Future partners such as campuses, venues, local communities, or municipalities.

## Brand Position

Parkio is not a navigation app, taxi brand, parking payment tool, or generic map clone. It is a parking intelligence layer focused on community-contributed availability, trust, privacy, and local density.

## Differentiators

- Community-powered spot sharing.
- Photo-backed submissions.
- Verification, claiming, reports, and moderation.
- Trust and contribution signals.
- Mobile-first contribution.
- Privacy-conscious architecture.
- Technically mature release-candidate application layer.

## Stage Discipline

Parkio should sound ambitious but not launched beyond evidence.

Allowed:

- "Release candidate."
- "Hosted-beta preparation."
- "Application layer certified for hosted-beta use."
- "Public production not ready."

Avoid:

- "Publicly launched."
- "Production-ready."
- "Trusted by users."
- "Partnered with cities."
- "Accepted by AWS/Microsoft/Google."
- "Guaranteed parking."

## Brand North Star

Clear parking decisions through trusted community signals.
