# Marketing Style

## Marketing Position

Parkio marketing should make the product understandable within 30 seconds:

- Parking is uncertain.
- Drivers can share and verify real spots.
- Parkio turns that into trusted parking intelligence.
- The product is in hosted-beta preparation.

## Page Style

- Direct hero.
- Clear problem statement.
- Concrete workflow.
- Evidence-backed trust section.
- Technical credibility without overwhelming the reader.
- Honest beta status.

## Pitch Deck Style

Recommended flow:

1. Problem.
2. Insight.
3. Solution.
4. Product workflow.
5. Why now.
6. Market hypothesis.
7. Technology credibility.
8. Beta plan.
9. Business model hypotheses.
10. Ask.

## Social Style

Post about:

- Product progress.
- Beta learnings.
- Technical readiness.
- Privacy and trust decisions.
- Parking pain insights.

Avoid:

- Fake traction.
- Growth claims without numbers.
- "Stealth mode" mystery language.
- Overly technical threads as the only communication style.

## Screenshot Style

- Use real product screens when available.
- Use demo data only.
- Avoid showing real personal data or precise private locations.
- Prefer clean light mode screens.
- Annotate sparingly.

## Demo Video Style

- Short, workflow-based.
- Show share, discover, verify/claim, report, and trust.
- Include beta caveat where relevant.
- Avoid cinematic car footage that hides the product.
