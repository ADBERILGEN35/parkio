# Photography Style

## Role of Photography

Photography should make Parkio feel grounded in real parking situations. It should show actual context rather than abstract mobility stock imagery.

## Recommended Subjects

- Urban curbside parking.
- Campus or commuter parking areas.
- Drivers near parked cars, without showing private plate details prominently.
- Phone-in-hand moments showing parking context.
- Street-level context with visible but non-dominant signage.
- Calm daylight city scenes.

## Photo Principles

- Realistic, not staged luxury.
- Bright natural light.
- Human but not crowded.
- Contextual enough to understand parking.
- Avoid identifiable private data where possible.

## Avoid

- Taxi/rideshare scenes.
- Dark parking garages as primary imagery.
- Aggressive traffic or crash imagery.
- Blurry city bokeh with no parking relevance.
- Luxury car glamour shots.
- Surveillance-camera aesthetics.
- Photos that reveal license plates or faces unnecessarily.

## Composition

- Leave clean negative space for text overlays.
- Use shallow depth of field sparingly.
- Favor horizontal hero crops and vertical mobile crops.
- Keep product screenshots separate from lifestyle imagery.

## Privacy Treatment

- Blur or avoid readable plates in public assets.
- Avoid showing precise home locations.
- Do not imply background tracking.
- Use generic demo data in screenshots.
