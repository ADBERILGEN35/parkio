# Logo Direction

Do not generate logo graphics in this sprint. This document defines direction for future design work.

## Logo Goals

The Parkio logo should communicate:

- Parking.
- Location intelligence.
- Community trust.
- Calm reliability.
- Modern mobility.

It should not look like:

- A taxi company.
- A cryptocurrency token.
- A game app.
- A generic map pin app.
- A government parking authority.

## Concept Directions

### Concept A: P Spot Marker

A simple `P` integrated with a parking bay or rounded location marker. The marker should be subtle so it does not become a generic map-pin logo.

### Concept B: Verified Parking Bay

A minimal top-down parking bay shape with a small check or signal point. This emphasizes verified availability and avoids map-clone visuals.

### Concept C: Community Signal

Three small signal points converging around a central parking mark. This emphasizes community-powered intelligence.

## Shape Language

- Rounded but not playful.
- Geometric but not cold.
- Clear at small sizes.
- Balanced positive and negative space.
- Avoid complex route lines inside the logo.

## Wordmark

- Wordmark text: `Parkio`.
- Prefer sentence case, not all caps.
- Letter spacing should be normal.
- Use custom refinement only if it improves readability.

## Lockups

Required variants:

- Horizontal lockup: symbol left, wordmark right.
- Stacked lockup: symbol above wordmark.
- Symbol-only mark.
- Wordmark-only mark.

## Spacing and Safe Area

- Minimum safe area: at least the width of the symbol's internal counter or 25 percent of symbol width, whichever is larger.
- Do not place the logo too close to map lines, photos, or busy backgrounds.
- Use solid backgrounds or calm surfaces behind the mark.

## Minimum Size

- Full horizontal logo: 120 px wide minimum on digital.
- Symbol-only: 24 px minimum on digital.
- Favicon: test at 16 px, 32 px, and 48 px.

## Responsive Variants

- Desktop nav: horizontal lockup.
- Mobile nav: symbol plus optional wordmark if space permits.
- App icon/favicons: symbol-only.
- Social avatar: symbol-only on Parkio Teal or Warm Off White.

## Monochrome Usage

The logo must work in:

- Parkio Teal on light background.
- White on Parkio Teal.
- Ink on light background.
- White on dark background.

Avoid gradients inside the logo.

## Favicon Ideas

- `P` inside a simplified parking bay.
- Verified parking bay symbol.
- Symbol-only community signal mark.

Favicon must remain recognizable without text.
