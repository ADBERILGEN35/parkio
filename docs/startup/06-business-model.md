# Business Model

## Current Revenue Status

- Revenue: none documented.
- Paid conversion: Not yet measured.
- Willingness to pay: Not yet measured.
- Partnerships: none documented.

Parkio should treat monetization as a post-beta validation question. The immediate business objective is to prove that drivers contribute and consume useful parking intelligence in a constrained geography.

## Monetization Hypotheses

These are options to validate, not current claims.

### Freemium

Keep core discovery and contribution flows free during beta. Later, evaluate paid convenience features only if users demonstrate repeated value.

### Premium Smart Return and Alerts

Potential paid features:

- Advanced return-time reminders.
- Higher-priority or more granular alerts.
- Saved parking zones.
- Personalized notification preferences.

Status: TBD. Not monetized during beta.

### Local Partnerships

Possible future partner types:

- Campuses.
- Venues.
- Employers with commuter parking pressure.
- Local businesses in parking-constrained areas.

Status: no partnerships documented.

### Municipal or Campus Dashboards

Potential future product:

- Aggregated parking availability trends.
- Moderation and report insights.
- Area-level demand signals.

Status: future concept only. Requires privacy review, aggregation thresholds, and public-sector or campus validation.

### B2B Analytics

Potential future product:

- Aggregated, non-identifying parking demand and contribution trends.
- Operational dashboards for private campuses, venues, or employers.

Status: future concept only.

## What Is Not Monetized During Beta

- Selling user location history.
- Selling raw individual movement data.
- Selling user identities or contact information.
- Paid claims of official availability.
- Municipal or enforcement workflows.

## Privacy-Safe Monetization Principles

- Use aggregation and minimization.
- Do not expose precise individual movement patterns.
- Keep spot-level public data separate from sensitive user history.
- Require opt-in and clear terms for any premium personal features.
- Prefer product value over surveillance-based monetization.
