# Target Market

## Primary User

Parkio's primary user is a driver who regularly parks in a dense area where availability is uncertain and timing matters.

Examples:

- Urban drivers searching for street, public-lot, or neighborhood parking.
- Commuters parking near work, transit, campus, or dense districts.
- Visitors to unfamiliar areas where parking rules and availability are unclear.
- Drivers willing to contribute spot observations in exchange for better local intelligence.

## Early Adopters

The best early adopters are not every driver in a city. They are drivers in one constrained geography who both feel parking pain and are willing to contribute:

- Campus drivers.
- Dense-neighborhood commuters.
- Local community members already sharing parking tips informally.
- Drivers who frequently arrive during peak parking pressure.
- Beta testers comfortable reporting bugs, false positives, and confusing flows.

The beta should start in one constrained area rather than claiming broad city coverage. The goal is to learn whether Parkio can create useful local density, not to maximize geography before the contribution loop is proven.

## Beta Tester Profile

Ideal beta testers:

- Drive and park frequently in the target area.
- Can test both sharing and discovery flows.
- Are willing to upload real spot photos where appropriate.
- Can report trace IDs, screenshots, device/browser details, and expected vs actual behavior.
- Understand that Parkio is in hosted-beta preparation, not public production.

## Expansion Path

1. Internal/local validation.
2. Operator-run hosted beta.
3. Beta cohort 1 in one constrained geography.
4. Beta cohort 2 in a nearby or similar area if density and reliability improve.
5. Public beta after production hardening gates improve.
6. Longer-term campus, venue, employer, municipal, or local-business partnerships only after real usage data supports the use case.

## Excluded For Now

- Public citywide production users.
- Official enforcement or compliance workflows.
- Paid parking inventory operators requiring contractual integrations.
- Municipal dashboards as the primary beta product.
- High-risk legal or parking advice claims.
