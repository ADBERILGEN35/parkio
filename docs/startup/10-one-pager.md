# Parkio One-Pager

## Parkio

Community-powered parking intelligence for real-world availability.

## What It Is

Parkio helps drivers find, share, verify, and manage parking spots using community contributions, photos, trust signals, moderation, notifications, and mobile-first workflows.

## Problem

Drivers can route to a destination, but they often cannot know whether a nearby spot is available, legal, recent, and suitable for their vehicle. Useful local parking observations disappear unless they are captured and verified quickly.

## Solution

Parkio lets drivers upload spot photos, discover nearby spots, verify availability, claim spots after parking, report bad data, and build contributor trust over time. The product is designed for local density: one constrained beta geography first, then measured expansion.

## Why Now

Drivers already use mobile maps and community information, but real-time parking availability remains fragmented. A mobile-first contribution loop can turn fresh observations into structured parking intelligence.
