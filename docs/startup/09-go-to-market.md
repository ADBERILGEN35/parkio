# Go To Market

## Strategy

Parkio should not launch broadly first. Parking intelligence needs local density, so the go-to-market motion should begin with one constrained geography where the same people can contribute, discover, verify, claim, and report spots often enough to test the loop.

## Launch Sequence

1. Complete hosted-beta infrastructure and DNS for `parkio.dev`.
2. Run operator preflight, deploy, HTTPS smoke, backup/restore, observability, and mobile device checks.
3. Open a private beta waitlist or direct invite path.
4. Recruit a small cohort in one constrained geography.
5. Validate contribution, discovery, verification, claim, report, notification, and moderation loops.
6. Expand only after quality, retention, and operational signals justify it.
7. Prepare public beta after production hardening gates improve.

## Early Beta Channels

- Direct outreach to drivers in the selected geography.
- University or campus communities.
- Local commuter groups.
- Neighborhood groups where parking pain is visible.
- Friends-and-family style testers who can give structured feedback.
- Developer/community updates that link to technical documentation without claiming public launch.

## Early Segment Hypotheses

### Campus Drivers

Campus parking is repeated, localized, and time-sensitive. This is a hypothesis to validate, not a proven segment.

### Urban Commuters

Commuters can test recurring parking behavior, Smart Return, and notification quality. This is a hypothesis to validate with beta usage.

### Local Communities

Local communities can create density because contributors and seekers operate in the same area. Parkio should start where spot observations can become useful quickly.

## Feedback Loops

- In-app visible errors with trace IDs.
- Beta feedback template.
- Structured tester questions.
- Manual follow-up on failed uploads, bad spots, reports, and confusing flows.
- Weekly review of support issues, metrics, and moderation load.

## Referral and Community Growth

Potential future loops:

- Invite nearby drivers after a tester successfully uses a spot.
- Reward useful verified contributions.
- Highlight trusted contributors.
- Share beta updates publicly without claiming public launch.

## Success Metrics

Metrics to measure:

- Waitlist signups.
- Activated beta users.
- Spot uploads.
- Upload-to-visible spot success rate.
- Verification rate.
- Claim rate.
- Report rate and moderation outcomes.
- Notification opt-in and engagement.
- Smart Return opt-in and successful reminders.
- Day 1, day 7, and day 30 retention.
- Support tickets per tester.
- System uptime and incident count.
